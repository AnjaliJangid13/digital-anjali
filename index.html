SEO Vocabulary

Understand common SEO terms in simple language

SEO

Search Engine Optimization is the process of improving a website's visibility in organic search results.

Keyword

A word or phrase people use when searching for information, products or services.

Backlink

A link from another website that points to a page on your website.

Organic Traffic

Visitors who reach a website through unpaid search engine results.

Search Intent

The main purpose behind a user's search query, such as learning or finding a website.

Meta Description

A short summary of a webpage that search engines may use in search result snippets.
